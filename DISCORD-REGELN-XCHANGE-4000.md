# Xchange – Community Rules

Welcome to **Xchange**, the official community of **Xyntal** – the platform where developers, designers, founders and everyone who wants to build something find their team and start projects together. By staying here you accept these rules, Discord's Terms of Service and Discord's Community Guidelines.

## How we treat each other
**1 · Respect first.** Treat everyone like a potential co-founder. No insults, harassment, threats, hate speech or discrimination. Criticise ideas, never people.
**2 · Every level is welcome.** No gatekeeping and no mocking of beginner questions. Nobody owes you free work or an instant reply – be patient and say thanks.
**3 · Feedback that helps.** Be honest, specific and constructive: what doesn't work, why, and what you'd try instead.

## Building together
**4 · Ideas belong to their owners.** Don't copy, sell or republish decks, designs or code that others share. Only post content you have the rights to. Public channels are public – keep confidential things private.
**5 · Transparent team search.** Post in #team-search with project, stage, roles, time commitment and compensation (paid, equity or voluntary). No fake projects, no misleading promises, no hidden unpaid trial work.
**6 · Deals are your responsibility.** Xyntal is not a party to agreements between members. Put important things in writing and be careful with money sent upfront.

## Promotion and spam
**7 · Promotion where it belongs.** Show your project in #showcase. No referral links or server invites without mod approval.
**8 · No spam.** No flooding, copypasta, cross-posting or needless pings.
**9 · No unsolicited DMs.** Don't DM members to sell, pitch or recruit. MLM, "get rich quick" offers and trading signals are forbidden.

## Privacy and security
**10 · Protect personal data.** No doxxing and no screenshots of private chats without consent.
**11 · Keep secrets secret.** Never post passwords, API keys or tokens. If it happens, delete the message and rotate the key.
**12 · No scams, phishing or malware.** No fake investments, jobs or giveaways, no advance-payment fraud, no phishing links, fake logins, QR-code logins, token grabbers, malicious files or hacked accounts – in channels and in DMs. The Xyntal team will never ask for your password, a login code or a payment in a DM.
**13 · Minimum age 16.** Like Xyntal itself, Xchange is for people aged 16+.

## Content and channels
**14 · Safe for everyone.** No NSFW, violent, shocking or illegal content – including names, avatars and statuses.
**15 · Stay on topic.** Use the right channel and threads. Heated debates about politics and religion don't belong here.
**16 · Language.** English is the main language; German is welcome in the German channels.
**17 · AI content.** AI tools are welcome, but don't flood channels with unedited output. You are responsible for what you post.
**18 · Voice.** The same rules apply. No recording without everyone's consent.

## Moderation
**19 · No impersonation.** Don't pose as another member, a moderator or the Xyntal team.
**20 · Listen to the mods.** They have the final say. Disagree? Open a ticket in #support instead of arguing in public. Report problems rather than confronting people yourself.
**21 · No ban evasion.** Second accounts used to dodge a timeout or ban are banned permanently.

## Consequences
Depending on severity: **warning → timeout → kick → ban.**

**Zero tolerance – immediate permanent ban without warning:**
• scams and fraud of any kind
• phishing, malware, token grabbers
• hacked accounts, raids, spam bots
• impersonating the Xyntal team or mods
• doxxing and threats
• hate speech
• illegal content
• ban evasion

These cases are also reported to Discord. You may appeal a decision once via a ticket in #support.

**Report:** ping @Moderator or open a ticket in #support. Reports are handled confidentially.
