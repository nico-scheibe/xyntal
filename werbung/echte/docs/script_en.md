# Xyntal – "The Real Ones" · Script (English, draft)

9:16 · ~38 s · same beats as the reference, rewritten in our own words and turned toward Xyntal at the end.
No swearing: TikTok's ad policy doesn't allow profanity in ad creatives.

Look from the reference: big caps captions at the top that build word by word, glowing figures on a dark floor,
cross-outs, opinion chips ("get a real job"), an "APPROVAL 0 / 100" counter, the phone switching off, a tower of
blocks you stack yourself. Twist: other builders light up, the tower goes up together, Xyntal connects them.

|---|---|---|---|
