# 📜 Xchange – Community Rules

Welcome to **Xchange**, the official community of **Xyntal** – the platform where developers, designers, founders and everyone who wants to build something find their team and start projects together.

Xchange is where that happens in real time: you share ideas, get feedback, find co-founders, show what you're building and help others do the same. To keep it a place where people actually want to share their work, we have a few rules.

**By staying on this server you agree to:**
• these rules,
• [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms),
• [Discord's Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).

Not knowing the rules is not an excuse. Please read them once – it takes three minutes.

<!-- ✂️ MESSAGE 2 -->
## 🤝 Part 1 – How we treat each other

### 1 · Respect first
Treat everyone like a potential co-founder – because they might be.
• No insults, harassment, bullying, threats or personal attacks.
• No hate speech or discrimination based on origin, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, age or anything else.
• Criticise ideas, never people. "This pricing model won't work because …" is fine. "You're clueless" is not.
• "It was just a joke" does not make something okay.

### 2 · Every level is welcome
Xchange is for first-time builders and experienced founders alike.
• No gatekeeping, no mocking beginner questions.
• If a question has been asked before, link the answer instead of being condescending.
• Nobody owes you free work or an instant reply. Be patient, and say thanks when someone helps.

### 3 · Feedback that helps
• Be honest, specific and constructive: say what doesn't work, why, and what you'd try instead.
• If you ask for feedback, expect honest feedback – and take it without getting defensive.
• Don't give feedback nobody asked for on people's personal choices. Stick to the work.

<!-- ✂️ MESSAGE 3 -->
## 🚀 Part 2 – Building together

### 4 · Ideas and work belong to their owners
People share early ideas, pitch decks, designs and code here. That only works with trust.
• Don't copy, sell, republish or pass off someone else's work as your own.
• Don't use what someone shared here to build a competing copy behind their back.
• Only post content you have the rights to. No pirated software, leaked courses or cracked tools.
• Keep in mind: a public channel is public. Don't post anything that must stay confidential – use private conversations and, if needed, an NDA for that.

### 5 · Team search – be transparent
Looking for co-founders or team members? Use **#team-search** and include:
• what the project is and what stage it's at,
• which roles you're looking for,
• expected time commitment,
• compensation: **paid, equity, revenue share or voluntary** – say it clearly.

Not allowed: fake projects, misleading promises ("guaranteed income"), hidden unpaid "trial tasks", or recruiting for something other than what you describe. One post per project – update it instead of reposting.

### 6 · Deals between members are your responsibility
Xyntal and the mod team are not a party to agreements between members. We don't mediate payments, equity splits or contracts. Put important things in writing, check who you're working with, and be careful with money sent upfront.

<!-- ✂️ MESSAGE 4 -->
## 📣 Part 3 – Promotion, spam and DMs

### 7 · Promotion only where it belongs
We want to see what you're building – in the right place.
• Show your project in **#showcase** or **#projects**. Tell us what it is and what you learned – don't just drop a link.
• No promotion in other channels, and no repeated posts for the same thing.
• No referral links, affiliate links or invites to other Discord servers without mod approval.

### 8 · No spam
• No flooding, copypasta, excessive caps or emoji walls.
• No unnecessary pings. Never use @everyone / @here, and don't ping team members for things a ticket can solve.
• Don't cross-post the same message in several channels.

### 9 · No unsolicited DMs
• Don't DM members to sell something, pitch a service or recruit – ask publicly first and let people opt in.
• Strictly forbidden: MLM / pyramid schemes, "get rich quick" offers, crypto or trading signals, paid "mentoring" pitches, fake giveaways.
• If someone DMs you like this, report it to the mods with a screenshot.

<!-- ✂️ MESSAGE 5 -->
## 🔒 Part 4 – Privacy and security

### 10 · Protect personal data
• No doxxing: never share someone's real name, address, phone number, workplace, photos or other personal data without their consent.
• Don't post screenshots of private conversations without permission from everyone in them.
• Don't post your own Xyntal account details publicly.

### 11 · Keep secrets secret
• Never post passwords, API keys, access tokens or `.env` files – not even "just for a second". If it happens, delete the message **and rotate the key immediately**.
• Asking for help with code? Remove secrets first.

### 12 · No scams, phishing or malware – instant ban
• No scams of any kind: fake investments, fake jobs, fake giveaways, advance-payment fraud, "send me money / crypto and I'll double it".
• No phishing links, fake login pages, QR-code logins, token grabbers, malicious files or suspicious downloads.
• No link shorteners that hide where a link goes.
• No hacked or stolen accounts, and no selling or trading of accounts.
• This applies in channels **and in DMs to members**. One attempt is enough: **permanent ban, no warning.**
• **The Xyntal team will never ask for your password, a login code or a payment in a DM.** Anyone who does is not us – report them.

### 13 · Minimum age 16
Like Xyntal itself, Xchange is for people aged **16 and up** – and in any case at least the minimum age Discord requires in your country. Accounts that are clearly younger will be removed.

<!-- ✂️ MESSAGE 6 -->
## 🧭 Part 5 – Content and channels

### 14 · Safe for everyone
• No NSFW, sexual, violent or shocking content – this includes profile pictures, usernames, statuses and banners.
• No illegal content and no instructions for illegal activities.
• No glorification of violence, extremism, self-harm or drug abuse.

### 15 · Stay on topic, use the right channel
• Read the channel description before posting.
• Off-topic talk goes to **#off-topic**.
• Use threads for longer discussions so channels stay readable.
• Heated debates about politics and religion don't belong here – this is a builder community.

### 16 · Language
• Main language is **English**. German is welcome in the dedicated German channels.
• Write clearly – others should be able to follow the conversation.

### 17 · AI-generated content
• AI tools are welcome – most of us use them.
• Don't flood channels with unedited AI output, and don't present AI-generated work as handmade when someone asks.
• You are responsible for what you post, no matter who or what wrote it.

### 18 · Voice channels
• The same rules apply in voice as in text.
• No recording or streaming others without the consent of everyone in the channel.
• No soundboard spam, no screaming, no deliberately disruptive noise.

<!-- ✂️ MESSAGE 7 -->
## 🛡️ Part 6 – Moderation

### 19 · Names and impersonation
• Use a readable, mentionable name – no invisible characters or offensive names.
• Don't pretend to be another member, a moderator or part of the Xyntal team. Official roles are only given by the team.

### 20 · Listen to the mods
• Moderators have the final say in the moment.
• Disagree with a decision? Open a ticket in **#support** and explain calmly. Don't argue about it in public channels.
• No "mini-modding": if you see a problem, report it instead of confronting the person yourself.
• Don't look for loopholes. The rules are meant in spirit, not just in wording – if something obviously harms the community, it isn't allowed just because it isn't listed.

### 21 · No ban evasion
Using a second account to get around a timeout or ban leads to a permanent ban of all accounts.

### 📨 How to report
• Right-click the message → **Apps / Report**, or ping **@Moderator**, or open a ticket in **#support**.
• Add a screenshot or message link where possible.
• Reports are handled confidentially. False reports made on purpose are a rule violation themselves.

## ⚖️ Consequences

Depending on how serious it is, and whether it happened before:

**1. Warning** – a reminder of the rule.
**2. Timeout** – temporarily muted (from hours up to several days).
**3. Kick** – removed from the server; you may rejoin.
**4. Ban** – permanently removed.

🚫 **Zero tolerance – immediate permanent ban, no warning:**
• scams and fraud of any kind
• phishing, malware, token grabbers, fake login pages
• hacked accounts, raids, spam bots
• impersonating the Xyntal team or moderators
• doxxing and threats
• hate speech
• illegal content, sexual content involving minors
• ban evasion

These cases are also reported to Discord, and compromised accounts are removed until the owner has secured them. Where a violation also involves a Xyntal account (e.g. scamming other members), the Xyntal account may be reviewed too.

**Appeals:** Think a decision was wrong? You can appeal once via a ticket in **#support**, or by e-mail if you've been banned. Appeals are reviewed by a team member who wasn't involved in the original decision where possible.

## 🔗 Help with Xyntal
Problems with your Xyntal account, profile or projects? Open a ticket in **#support** – please never post account data in public channels. Feature ideas and bug reports go to **#feedback**.
